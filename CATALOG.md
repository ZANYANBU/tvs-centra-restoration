# TVS Centra Restoration — Photo Journal

A photo-by-photo record of bringing my father's TVS Centra back to life, grouped by the stage of the build.


## Before the rescue

### Worn black Centra, left side before restoration

![Worn black Centra, left side before restoration](photos/01-before-the-rescue/01-worn-black-centra-left-side-before-restoration-IMG_6364.jpg)

A complete black TVS Centra photographed in left-side profile against a stone-tile wall. The seat is torn with foam bursting out, and there is surface rust on the rear grab rail and frame. Chrome front forks, spoked wheels, the engine, and exhaust are all intact. A TVS logo is on the fuel tank and a 'VTi' graphic is on the side panel. The bike shows heavy, aged use.
