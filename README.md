# 🏍️ TVS Centra Restoration — Bringing Dad's Bike Back to Life

> Reviving the **TVS Centra** that carried my father for **20+ years and 60,000+ km**, using good parts from a **₹3,000 junkyard donor bike** — plus an LED headlight and a full AC→DC electrical conversion. A father-and-son project, done at home, in our spare time.

## The story

My father bought this TVS Centra back in **2004**. It carried him for more than **twenty years and over 60,000 km**. In its last couple of years the plastic **side panels cracked**, the **starter coil burnt out**, and the bike ended up **standing unused for over a year**, quietly gathering rust in the corner.

I didn't want it to end there — so I decided to bring it back.

I found a **second TVS Centra in a scrapyard** and bought it for just **₹3,000**. It had run only about **12,000 km** and was mechanically healthy, so it became the **donor**: I took its good parts, moved them onto my father's bike, upgraded the lighting, and rewired the whole electrical system. Somewhere in the middle of all that grease, I ended up learning how a lot of this old machinery actually works.

## What we did

**Parts moved from the donor ➜ Dad's bike**
- 🔌 **Starter coil** — replacing the burnt-out one that had stranded the bike
- 🎛️ **Front dash / speedometer console** — the whole instrument cluster
- 🦵 **Center stand**
- 🪝 **Two front fork suspensions**

**Upgrades & electrical work**
- 💡 Fitted a **modern LED headlight**
- ⚡ Converted the **headlamp and the entire electrical system from AC to DC**, so the lights glow steadily instead of pulsing with engine revs

**Salvaged & kept from the donor**
- 🛠️ The donor's **old engine** was removed and **preserved for future use**
- 🔧 The old **rusty silencer** was taken off and replaced

## 🔧 What I learned

This project taught me as much as it fixed. A few things that stuck:

- **Carburettor** — how it meters fuel and air, and how the choke and throttle change the mix.
- **Clutch plate** — how the friction plates engage and disengage the engine from the gearbox.
- **Starter coil** — diagnosing the burnt coil and swapping in the donor's good one.
- **AC vs DC lighting — the big one.** On the stock Centra the **headlamp runs on AC straight from the magneto coil**, so its brightness rises and falls with engine RPM and it won't glow with the engine off. **Converting the circuit to DC** (fed from the battery through the rectifier/regulator) gives a **steady, constant glow** at any RPM — and light even when idling.
- **Why an LED, not a halogen.** A **halogen headlamp bulb is ~30–65 W** and pulls **high current** — too much for the small battery once it's on DC. A **modern LED is only ~15–30 W**, draws far less, runs cool, and suits the converted system perfectly.

## About the bike

The **TVS Centra** is a 100cc, four-stroke commuter motorcycle from TVS Motor Company, launched around 2004 (it later became the TVS Star). Simple, economical, and built to run for decades — which is exactly what ours did.
